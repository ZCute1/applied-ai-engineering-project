## Exercise

Shibas are an active, alert breed that generally want 60 minutes or more of
activity daily, split across the day. They are climbers and escape artists when
bored, so consistent daily exercise does more for household peace than an
occasional long hike.

## Off-leash is risky

Shibas have a strong prey drive and independent recall — a bird or squirrel can
easily beat a well-practised "come". Off-leash time belongs in a securely fenced
area, and open-space walks are safer on a long line. Plan walks as leashed
outings unless you know the space is enclosed.

## Grooming and the coat blow

A Shiba's double coat sheds year-round and blows heavily for a few weeks in
spring and autumn. Weekly brushing is enough most of the year, but during a coat
blow daily 10 to 15 minute sessions keep the shedding manageable. The undercoat
should be brushed out, not shaved — the coat insulates against heat as well as
cold.

## Temperament

Shibas are famously self-possessed and dislike being fussed over on someone
else's schedule. Short, frequent training sessions land better than long ones,
and handling routines like nail trims go easier when they are practised in small
doses well before they are needed.
