## How much exercise

A high-energy adult dog generally needs 60 to 90 minutes of real activity a day —
working and sporting breeds often want more. Under-exercised dogs tend to show it
indoors: chewing, pacing, barking at nothing, and trouble settling in the evening.

## Split it across the day

Two or three shorter outings beat one long one. A 45-minute walk in the morning
and a 30-minute walk later in the day is easier on joints, spreads the dog's
mental stimulation across the whole day, and gives more chances to relieve
itself than a single big block does.

## Morning matters most

Most high-energy dogs are at their most restless right after waking. Getting the
longest walk in early sets up a calmer day, which is why a morning slot is worth
protecting when the schedule gets tight.

## Physical is not enough

Sniffing, chewing, training, and puzzle feeders tire a dog out in a way that
distance alone does not. Ten to fifteen minutes of scent work or training can take
the edge off a dog that has already been walked. Short enrichment sessions are
also easy to slot into small gaps in a busy day.
