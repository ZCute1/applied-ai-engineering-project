## How often to brush

A shorthaired cat — including most tabbies — does well with a brushing once or
twice a week, around 10 minutes at a time. Longhaired cats need it daily: their
coat mats within days, and mats have to be cut out rather than brushed through
once they form.

## Why it helps

Brushing removes the loose hair the cat would otherwise swallow while
self-grooming, which cuts down on hairballs. It is also the easiest way to notice
lumps, scabs, fleas, or sore spots early, simply because you are handling the
whole animal regularly.

## Keep sessions short

Most cats have a limit measured in minutes. Stopping while the cat is still
enjoying it keeps the next session easy; pushing past the point where the tail
starts flicking teaches the cat that grooming is something to escape.

## Nails and older cats

Indoor cats' claws need trimming every few weeks, since they do not wear down the
way an outdoor cat's do. Older cats also groom themselves less thoroughly as they
get stiffer, so a cat that always kept itself immaculate and has started looking
unkempt may need more help from you — and a check-up.
