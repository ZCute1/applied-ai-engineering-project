## Same times every day

Both dogs and cats settle better on a predictable feeding routine. Meals at
roughly the same times each day steady digestion and cut down on begging, and they
give the rest of the day's routine an anchor to hang off.

## How many meals

Most adult dogs do well on two meals a day, morning and evening. Cats naturally
eat many small meals, so two to four smaller portions suit them better than one
large one; puzzle feeders and timed feeders help spread it out without adding
work.

## Around exercise

Leave a gap between a full meal and hard exercise — feeding immediately before or
after a vigorous walk is best avoided, particularly for large dogs. Feeding after
the walk, once the dog has settled, is the usual pattern.

## Meals as leverage

Feeding time is the easiest routine to attach other tasks to: medication, a
weekly weigh-in, or a short training session all go more smoothly when a meal is
waiting at the end. Scheduling feeding first and building around it tends to work
better than fitting it in last.
